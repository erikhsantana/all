FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Orbit really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and covers everything most individuals need. Pro adds auto-scheduling and unlimited integrations.

Which calendars does Orbit work with?

Orbit connects to the major calendar providers and any calendar that supports standard CalDAV or ICS feeds.

Can I import tasks from another app?

You can import from CSV or connect your existing tools directly, and your tasks arrive with due dates and labels intact.

Is my data private?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell it and never show ads.

Can I cancel anytime?

Of course. Downgrade or cancel from settings in two clicks; you keep all your data on the Free plan.
